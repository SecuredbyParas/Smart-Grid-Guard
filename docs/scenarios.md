# Synthetic Scenarios

All data is generated by `src/generate_data.py` (random seed 42, so it is the same every time).
Period: 2 Nov 2026 to 15 Nov 2026, hourly. Days 1-10 are the "normal" baseline. Scenarios are in the last 4 days.

## Normal behaviour
- 60 meters, 15 per substation (SS-01 to SS-04), each with its own average consumption.
- Daily shape: small peak around 08:00, bigger peak around 19:00, plus 8% random noise.
- Voltage about 230 V, about 20 packets/hour, about 45 ms latency.
- Admin staff (op_ravi, op_meera, eng_sam) log in from internal `10.x.x.x` addresses during working hours.
- One planned firmware update on GW-02 during the day (6 Nov, 11:00). It must **not** be flagged.

## Injected scenarios

| ID | Date | What was injected | Expected detection |
|----|------|-------------------|--------------------|
| S1 | 12 Nov onward | Meter M-007 (SS-01) reads about 92% lower than normal. Looks like tampering or a bypass (energy theft). | Consumption drop, one meter, long duration |
| S2 | 13 Nov 14:00-17:00 | Voltage drops by 18 V on every meter in SS-02. | Voltage deviation on all 15 meters at one location, so more likely a grid fault than a cyber attack |
| S3 | 14 Nov 01:48-05:59 | Outside IP `203.0.113.45` fails to log in 6 times as `svc_backup`, then succeeds, changes config, creates a user and pushes firmware on GW-03 and M-033. From 02:00, meters M-033, M-036, M-040 send about 12x more packets and have +180 ms latency. | Admin alerts and communication anomalies in the same substation and time window, so one correlated Critical incident |
| S4 | 15 Nov 03:30 | 3 failed logins on GW-04 from `198.51.100.9`, nothing else happens. | Admin-only Low incident (nothing correlated) |

`data/ground_truth.csv` lists the injected events. `run_pipeline.py` compares detections with it.

## Result on this dataset (threshold 5.0)
- All 4 scenarios detected as 4 incidents.
- 0 abnormal readings outside the injected scenarios.
- This is a best-case result because the synthetic noise is simple. See `limitations.md`.
